usage: verify_liegroup_run.py [-h] [--source-root SOURCE_ROOT]
                              [--source-manifest SOURCE_MANIFEST] --output-dir
                              OUTPUT_DIR --manifold {SO3,SE3} --method
                              {ours,rcpm} [--gamma GAMMA] --gpu GPU

Record one native SO(3)/SE(3) experiment from the recovered paper runners.
Calls the unchanged builders, trainers and evaluators. One training seed and
five evaluation batches constitute a native row; these are not five training
replicates. RCPM gamma is selected only to distribute the original sweep
across fresh processes. No configuration, optimizer, loss or metric is
replaced.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --source-root SOURCE_ROOT
  --source-manifest SOURCE_MANIFEST
  --output-dir OUTPUT_DIR
                        Fresh directory for this one row.
  --manifold {SO3,SE3}
  --method {ours,rcpm}
  --gamma GAMMA         Required for RCPM; one of the recovered sweep values.
  --gpu GPU             One physical NVIDIA GPU index.
